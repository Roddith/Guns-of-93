# Guns of 93

## Features
- A huge assortment of firearms with updated models, icons, and removable parts/attachments.
- Magazines, ammunition, silencers, bayonets, and crafting recipes.
- Keep your weapons cleaned and repaired.

## Compatibility
- Other firearm mods *Are Likely* to conflict.  Use them at your own risk, unless they specifically claim to be compatible or dependent on Guns of 93
- Vanilla firearms are removed by default; this can be disabled in sandbox settings.
- Unsubscribing and resubscribing may help if Steam misses an update.

## Known multiplayer issues
- Icons may misrepresent weapon finishes or ammunition counts.
- Installing or removing upgrades may take repeated attempts.
- Guns displayed on zombies may differ from their eventual loot.
